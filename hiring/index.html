Research opportunities

Working with me

I dislike pushing people. I work best with highly self-motivated students who enjoy research itself and can take ownership of difficult, uncertain problems.

What I am looking for

  • Strong intrinsic motivation and the ability to make progress without continuous prompting.
  • Interest in secure and reliable AI agents, AI for security, trustworthy evidence, or closely related research.
  • A desire to work on something genuinely interesting where I can contribute intellectually.
  • Openness to a brief trial of approximately three months before making a longer commitment.
  • Honesty about failures, uncertainty, available time, and what you do not yet understand.

Possible routes

Possible routes include a University of Sydney Capstone, Research Pathway, Thesis, or a short PhD-style trial. Formal eligibility, unit requirements, workload, and deadlines depend on the exact route and must be checked against current official University information.
